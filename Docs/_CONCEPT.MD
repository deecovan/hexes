# Concept of Gameplay.

## Game parts:
1. Hexes Card Duel.
2. Walking and Collecting.
3. Camp Defence and Deck Masters.

## Game Mechanics:
1. Free and Greed Battle Area.
   * Free like MTG, with card's Price, Power and Health;
     - Dext used to strike order and attack or block repeating,
     - Ranged used as Flying.
   * Greed with Dext and Range parameters:
     - Dext to use as Action Points, to move cards,
     - Range to attack ranged hexes.
